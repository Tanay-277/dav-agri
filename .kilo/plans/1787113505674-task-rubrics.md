# Task Rubrics

## General Scoring Rules

- **Full credit (1 point):** Answer matches the correct option exactly.
- **Partial credit (0.5 points):** Answer is semantically equivalent but uses different wording.
- **No credit (0 points):** Answer is incorrect or participant says "I don't know."
- **Skipped:** Recorded as 0 points with reason (stuck, refused, timeout).
- **Facilitator-assisted:** Recorded separately; does not count toward primary metric.

## 9. Comprehension Quiz Items (Post-Task)

After all tasks, administer 4 comprehension questions. These test data literacy, not task-specific recall.

**Q1:** If the rain icon is red, what does that mean?

- a) Good rain
- b) Bad rain / too much or too little
- c) Normal rain
- d) I don't know

**Correct:** b

**Q2:** If the yield arrow points up, what does that mean?

- a) Yield is higher than last year
- b) Yield is lower than last year
- c) Yield is the same
- d) I don't know

**Correct:** a

**Q3:** If the soil moisture icon shows a dry soil, what should you do?

- a) Harvest
- b) Irrigate
- c) Spray pesticide
- d) I don't know

**Correct:** b

**Q4:** Which chart shows you how a value changes over time?

- a) Bar chart
- b) Pie chart
- c) Line chart
- d) I don't know

**Correct:** c

**Scoring:** 1 point per correct answer; total 0–4.

## 11. System Usability Scale (SUS)

Please rate how much you agree with each statement:

| # | Statement | Strongly Disagree | Disagree | Neutral | Agree | Strongly Agree |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | I think that I would like to use this system frequently | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| 2 | I found the system unnecessarily complex | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| 3 | I thought the system was easy to use | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| 4 | I think that I would need the support of a technical person to be able to use this system | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| 5 | I found the various functions in this system were well integrated | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| 6 | I thought there was too much inconsistency in this system | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| 7 | I would imagine that most people would learn to use this system very quickly | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| 8 | I found the system very cumbersome to use | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| 9 | I felt very confident using the system | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| 10 | I needed to learn a lot of things before I could get going with this system | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |

**Scoring:**
- For odd items (1,3,5,7,9): subtract 1 from raw score
- For even items (2,4,6,8,10): subtract raw score from 5
- Sum all 10 adjusted scores → range 0–100
- Multiply by 2.5 to get SUS score (0–100)
