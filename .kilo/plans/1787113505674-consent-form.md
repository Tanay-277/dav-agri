# Informed Consent Form — AgriStory Study

## Study Title

Voice-Interactive Data Storytelling System for Rural/Low-Literacy Populations

## Principal Investigator

[Name, Institution, Contact]

## Purpose

We are building a new tool that uses voice and pictures to help farmers understand weather and crop data. We want to test whether this tool is easier to use than traditional charts and graphs.

## What You Will Do

- Use a mobile app or website to answer 4–8 questions about weather and crops
- The session takes about 45–60 minutes
- You may use voice commands or touch the screen

## Data Collected

- Your responses to questions and surveys
- Voice transcripts (what you say is converted to text; voice recordings are NOT stored)
- Interaction logs (which buttons you press, which filters you use)
- Demographics (age range, education level, occupation)

**We do NOT collect:** your name, phone number, exact village name, or any personal identifiers.

## Risks

- You may feel frustrated if the system does not understand your voice. You can stop at any time.
- No physical or psychological risks are expected.

## Benefits

- You will learn about a new tool that may help you access agricultural information.
- You will receive [incentive] for your time.
- Your feedback will help improve tools for farmers.

## Confidentiality

- All data is anonymous. You will be assigned a random ID number.
- Data is stored securely and will be deleted after 6 months.
- Only the research team has access to the data.

## Voluntary Participation

- Your participation is completely voluntary.
- You may skip any question or stop at any time without penalty.
- You may request that your data be deleted at any time.

## Contact

If you have questions about this study, contact [PI name] at [email/phone].
