# Screening Questionnaire

## Purpose

To determine eligibility for the pilot study and assign participants to appropriate groups.

## Administration

- Read aloud by facilitator in participant's preferred language
- Record responses on paper form
- Do NOT provide explanations beyond the question text
- If participant does not understand a question, rephrase once; if still unclear, skip
