# Facilitator Training Materials

## 1. Facilitator Checklist

### Before Session

- [ ] Device charged (≥80%) and screen brightness set to maximum
- [ ] Browser: Chrome (recommended) or Edge; Safari not recommended
- [ ] App URL bookmarked and tested
- [ ] Consent forms printed (2 copies: participant + facilitator)
- [ ] Screening questionnaire printed
- [ ] Task log sheet printed
- [ ] Survey printed (or loaded on second device)
- [ ] Incentive prepared (cash/voucher)
- [ ] Recording consent obtained and documented
- [ ] Screen recording app activated
- [ ] Quiet room confirmed; doors closed if possible
- [ ] Backup device available

### During Session

- [ ] Read welcome script verbatim
- [ ] Obtain verbal and signed consent
- [ ] Complete demographic questionnaire
- [ ] Administer pre-session literacy self-rating
- [ ] Explain tutorial for assigned condition
- [ ] Start screen recording
- [ ] Read task instructions verbatim
- [ ] Do NOT provide hints or answer guidance
- [ ] Record task completion, time, errors
- [ ] If participant is stuck > 60s: offer to skip task
- [ ] After tasks: administer survey
- [ ] Debrief: ask 3 open-ended questions
- [ ] Provide incentive
- [ ] Thank participant

### After Session

- [ ] Save screen recording with participant ID
- [ ] Complete facilitator notes
- [ ] Flag any anomalies for PI review
- [ ] Upload data to secure storage within 24h

## 2. Welcome Script

"Hello, my name is [facilitator name]. Thank you for coming today. I am going to ask you to use a new tool that uses voice and pictures to help understand weather and crop data. The session will take about 45–60 minutes. You will answer some questions and then fill out a short survey. You can stop at any time. Do you have any questions before we begin?"

---

## 3. Consent Script (Simplified, to be read aloud)

"We are testing a new voice tool for farmers. You will answer questions about weather and crops. Your voice is converted to text. We do NOT record your voice. We do not ask for your name or phone number. You can stop anytime. You will receive [₹ amount] for your time. Do you agree to participate?"

---

## 4. Tutorial Scripts by Condition

### Condition A: Conventional Dashboard

"This is the normal dashboard. It shows charts and numbers. You can use the filters at the top to choose crop, state, and district. Look at the charts and answer the questions. You can touch the screen to explore."

### Condition B: Voice-Only

"This tool uses your voice. Press the microphone button and speak your question in [language]. For example, say 'Show me wheat data for Punjab.' The system will answer with voice. You can also touch the screen if you prefer."

### Condition C: Voice + Icons

"This tool uses your voice and pictures. Press the microphone button and speak. When the system answers, look for the highlighted icon. The icon helps you understand the answer. For example, a rain icon means rain is important."

### Condition D: Voice + Icons + Story

"This tool tells you a story. Press the microphone button and ask a question. The system will speak a short story about the data, using pictures to help. Listen carefully and answer the questions."

## 5. Standardized Prompts

| Situation | Prompt |
|---|---|
| Participant asks for help | "Try to answer on your own. If you are stuck, we can move to the next question." |
| Participant says "I don't know" | "That is okay. Let's move to the next question." |
| Participant is silent > 30s | "Take your time. Let me know when you are ready." |
| Participant is stuck > 60s | "Would you like to try the next question?" |
| Participant asks about correctness | "I cannot tell you if you are right or wrong. Please answer as best you can." |
| Participant asks to repeat | "I can repeat the question one more time. After that, we must move on." |
| Technical failure | "The system is not working. We will skip this question and continue." |

---

## 6. Prohibited Behaviors

- Do NOT explain charts or icons
- Do NOT rephrase task instructions
- Do NOT provide hints about correct answers
- Do NOT express approval/disapproval of responses
- Do NOT answer participant questions about the study hypothesis
- Do NOT use technical jargon (e.g., "yield anomaly," "correlation")

---

## 7. Session Anomaly Log

Record any of the following:

- [ ] Participant requested to stop early
- [ ] Technical failure (app crash, voice not working)
- [ ] Participant used facilitator language (not local language)
- [ ] Participant appeared to guess randomly
- [ ] Third party interrupted session
- [ ] Device ran out of battery
- [ ] Audio recording failed
- [ ] Screen recording failed

---

## 8. Facilitator Certification

To be certified, a facilitator must:

1. Read this training manual
2. Role-play 2 complete sessions with another facilitator as participant
3. Demonstrate correct use of standardized prompts
4. Pass a 10-question quiz on protocol
5. Shadow 2 live sessions (no participant interaction)
6. Lead 2 supervised sessions with PI observation
